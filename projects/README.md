# Projects

Three independent portfolio projects, each defined by a product requirements document (PRD). They are separate builds, not modules of one product.

## Status

| Project | Role areas | Status | PRD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semiconductor supplier and inventory decision lab | Supplier management, supply chain planning, purchasing and inventory analysis | Specified, not built | [01](01_Supplier_and_Inventory_PRD.md) |
| AI infrastructure commercial strategy workbench | Sales strategy and operations, business development | Specified, not built | [02](02_Commercial_Strategy_PRD.md) |
| Service operations incident workflow prototype | Field service and service operations | Specified, not built | [03](03_Service_Operations_PRD.md) |

A project moves to "Built" only after its acceptance criteria pass and the work has been demonstrated.

## Ground rules

- No project is affiliated with an employer. None uses employer data.
- Operating data is synthetic and labeled as such. Public evidence is stored separately with source URLs and dates.
- Simulated results are not real company savings.
