03 / THE BIGGER QUESTION

Faster games.
Better business?

01

More people can build

AI may help beginners create playable games. More releases alone don't prove that games get better.

02

From TikTok to Roblox

Short clips can bring attention. TikTok views aren't Roblox visits, and visits aren't purchases.

03

Money shapes game design

Creators can earn from purchases, but players deserve honest prices, especially on a platform used by young people.
