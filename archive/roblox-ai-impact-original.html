So what's the answer?

AI might make Roblox more accessible to first-time creators. But making more games doesn't automatically mean more players, better experiences, or more profit. The key questions are whether games keep players interested—and whether new activity is worth the added costs.

This independent case study is a work in progress. Planned next steps include creator interviews, additional quarters of Roblox financial data, and deeper research on game discovery and retention.
